Welcome to Arjun and Nupur's Family Tree

Here's how to find your way around.

  1. Click/tap any individual name to move that person to the center and see their immediate family (their parents, siblings, spouse/partner, children, direct aunts/uncles, and first cousins).
  2. Tap the Cousins toggle to remove/add cousins from the view to simplify things.
  3. Continue tapping to travel through the family tree, one person at a time. You can use the ← and → buttons (in the bottom-left) to go backward and forward.
  4. Use the search bar (in the top-right) to find anyone by name, or use Go to Arjun / Go to Nupur to jump home.
  5. Pinch, double-tap, or use +/− to zoom, and drag to move around.
You can see these instructions again anytime by tapping the ? button.
